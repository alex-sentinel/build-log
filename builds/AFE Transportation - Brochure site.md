# AFE Transportation - Brochure site
*Built:* September 2026

Fernando Hernandez runs a one truck courier and moving operation out of the Twin Cities Metro Area. He's been getting work through word of mouth but needed an actual presence online. No quote form just yet since he's still getting DOT and MC authority sorted out. So I deployed him a brochure site for now, to get the phone ringing.

The site is built in plain static HTML. No framework. I wrote the design choices and the copy written in first person as Fernando. I included that he is bilingual (Hablamos español) and have the site in the card layout. 

This is the second case study for hernandezsg.com. When Fernando gets his operating authority I'll add a quote form with automation on top of it like E's Sweet Cakes.
